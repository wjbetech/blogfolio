orbit. brings the essentials of solo project work into one focused workspace. Rather than splitting planning, task management, and notes across separate tools, it keeps them together so the work is easier to follow.

Use it to organize projects, move tasks through a Kanban board, and keep supporting notes close at hand. orbit. takes cues from flexible workspaces like Notion and structured project tools like Jira, then pares the experience down for one person.
