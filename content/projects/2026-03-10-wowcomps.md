wowcomps is a raid-composition planner for the Classic World of Warcraft expansions. Pick your expansion and raid size, drag character specialisations onto a visual raid grid, rename slots to match your group, and see at a glance which buffs and debuffs your composition covers. Built around a hobby with drag-and-drop (dnd-kit), it turns raid planning into a quick, visual workflow.
